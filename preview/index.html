DEADSCROLL

Nudges, not blocks.

DEADSCROLL is an Android app that interrupts compulsive scrolling in the apps you choose, before it hardens into lost time. Instead of blocking those apps, it steps in with progressively stronger nudges: just enough pause to notice what you’re doing and choose whether to keep going.

Built on the idea that blockers rarely stick, because people learn to get around them. Live on Google Play, with your detailed history kept on your device.

UpLevel

Interview prep without the guesswork.

Interview preparation can be chaotic and rudderless. UpLevel does the heavy lifting of researching a company’s hiring culture and patterns, replacing generic advice with structured intelligence and graded mock interviews. It is built to strip away friction and give people their confidence back.
